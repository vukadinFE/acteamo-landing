We built Acteamo around a quiet, slightly radical idea: **tasks should wait for
you, not the other way around.**

## The problem with red badges

A red "12 overdue" badge does not make you more productive. It makes you anxious.
Over time the overdue pile becomes background noise you learn to ignore — which
defeats the entire purpose of tracking anything at all.

## Calm by default

Instead of alarms, Acteamo uses gentle nudges:

1. You set your capacity.
2. You get a list that fits.
3. You work and rest in a steady rhythm.

When the day is done, whatever is left simply waits. No penalty. No guilt.

## What you get instead

- **No due dates** — nothing is ever late.
- **No red notifications** — calm guidance replaces the alarms.
- **No time tracking** — just focus and rest.

Less pressure, by design.
