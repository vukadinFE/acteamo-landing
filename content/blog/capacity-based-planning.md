Most planning tools assume every day is the same. You are either "productive" or
you are "behind". Acteamo starts somewhere gentler: **how much capacity do you
have today?**

![The Acteamo capacity selector set to "Good"](/images/capacity-selector.svg)

## One tap sets the tone

When you open Acteamo you answer a single question — Drained, Low, Good or
Charged. That choice shapes everything that follows:

- On a **drained** day you might see one small thing.
- On a **charged** day the list opens up to match your energy.

Nothing is forced. The list is a suggestion, never an order.

::callout{type="tip" title="Try it yourself"}
Open the app and change your capacity. Watch the suggested list grow and shrink
to match how you feel — no commitment required.
::

## Why capacity beats deadlines

Deadlines create pressure even when they are self-imposed. Capacity planning
flips the question from *"what is overdue?"* to *"what fits right now?"*. The
result is a list you can actually finish — and finishing feels good.

> A good day is just enough on your list.

Give it a try and notice how the day feels lighter when nothing is ever late.
