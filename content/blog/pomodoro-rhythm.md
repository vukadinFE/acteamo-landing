The pomodoro technique is simple: work in focused blocks, then take a real break.
Acteamo bakes it right into your day so you never have to think about it.

## The loop

- **Focus** for 25 minutes.
- **Rest** for 5.
- Repeat as many times as your capacity allows.

That is the whole method. The magic is in the rest — short breaks keep your
attention fresh so the next focus block is easier.

## Make it yours

You do not have to finish every block. Skip, pause or stop whenever you like.
The timer is a companion, not a boss. If a short rest sounds good after two
tasks, take it.

Work. Rest. Repeat. That is enough.
