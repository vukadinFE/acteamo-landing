Acteamo is a **stress-free daily planner**. Instead of an endless to-do list with
looming deadlines, you tell Acteamo how much capacity you have today and it
suggests a list of tasks that fits.

## Our principles

- **Capacity first.** The day starts with how you feel, not what is overdue.
- **Suggestions, not orders.** AI proposes a list; you stay in control.
- **Calm by design.** No due dates, no red notifications, no time tracking.
- **Privacy you can trust.** Swiss-hosted and fully GDPR-compliant.

## How it works

1. Set your capacity — Drained, Low, Good or Charged.
2. Get a list that fits your day.
3. Work and rest with a built-in pomodoro rhythm.
